## Nine cars waiting on usable specs

Each list of ratios runs from first gear to top gear.

### 1997 Acura Integra Type R — exact US factory source

- B18C5 at 195 SAE hp @ 8,000 rpm and 130 lb-ft @ 7,500 rpm. Redline 8,400 rpm, fuel cut 8,500 rpm. 5MT ratios **3.250 / 2.105 / 1.458 / 1.107 / 0.848**, final drive **4.400**. OE tires **195/55 R15 Bridgestone RE010**. Use the Type R file because the general Integra file includes unrelated GS-R ratios. [Acura Type R US spec sheet](https://www.auto-brochures.com/makes/acura/integra/Acura_US%20Integra-R_1997.pdf)
- C/D weighed its car at **2,560 lb**, recorded **6.6 s to 60 mph**, and **15.2 s at 93 mph** in the quarter mile. The test describes a relative drag reduction versus GS-R, which gives no absolute Cd. [Original C/D test](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a15141504/1997-acura-integra-type-r-archived-test-review/)

### 2022 Audi RS 3 sedan — forward ratios verified, per-gear final-drive mapping still open

- Exact 2022 US spec listing and later pre-facelift Audi sedan engineering sheet agree on forward ratios to published rounding. Audi gives **3.560 / 2.530 / 1.680 / 1.020 / 0.790 / 0.760 / 0.630**. The EU table lists **4.059** and the US database lists **3.45**. Together they suggest two output ratios, but the documents checked do not establish their assignment for this US gearbox. Confirm the gearbox code and shaft mapping before using TT RS mapping or applying a single value to all gears. [2022 US model specs](https://www.caranddriver.com/audi/rs3/specs/2022/audi_rs3_audi-rs3_2022) · [Audi pre-facelift EU technical data](https://www.audi.com/system/production/car_motorizations/468/file_en/1d135b0858d0dbde3c70b0690ae8652197730017/eTD-Audi-RS3-Sedan-TFSI_231027.pdf?1698933729=&disposition=attachment)
- Audi's EU aero reference is **Cd 0.32 with 2.22 m² frontal area**. Keep the EU rating separate from US advertised power. C/D recorded **3,639 lb**, **3.3 s to 60 mph**, **11.8 s at 117 mph**, omitting **0.3 s** of one-foot rollout. The original test lists wheel and tire options. [Original C/D test](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a40157829/2022-audi-rs3-sedan-us-drive/)

### 2018 Audi TT RS coupe — both output-shaft ratios found

- Audi's launch-era **294 kW 8S coupe** technical sheet gives **3.563 / 2.526 / 1.679 / 1.022 / 0.788 / 0.761 / 0.635**. **4.059** applies in gears **1, 4, 5 and reverse**, and **3.450** in **2, 3, 6 and 7**. The exact MY2018 US C/D spec sheet corroborates all forward ratios to rounding and one final drive. Label the per-gear mapping as factory EU with US corroboration. Audi's EU aero values are **Cd 0.32 and 2.09 m²**. EU torque is **480 Nm @ 1,700–5,850 rpm**. Keep the US test engine rating and tires with the US car. [Audi launch-era technical sheet](https://sport-auto.ch/essais/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/etd-TT-RS-Coup%C3%A9-2.5-TFSI-294-kW_0716.pdf) · [MY2018 US model specs](https://www.caranddriver.com/audi/tt-rs/specs/2018/audi_tt-rs_audi-tt-rs-coupe_2018)
- C/D measured **3,270 lb**, **3.4 s to 60 mph**, **11.9 s at 117 mph**. [Original C/D test](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a14516384/2018-audi-tt-rs-test-review/)

### 2018 Alpine A110 Première Édition — test recovered and effective gearing available

- Alpine's exact-edition launch spec table lists **1,103 kg DIN**, **252 PS @ 6,000 rpm** and **320 Nm**. The same press release quotes 1,080 kg excluding options. Quattroruote weighed its car at **1,205 kg in test condition**, with **43/57** weight distribution. Its road aero figures are **Cd 0.338, area 1.918 m²**. Test results are **4.9 s 0–100 km/h**, **13.0 s at 176.7 km/h across 400 m**, and **23.7 s at 220.7 km/h across 1 km**. Preserve 400 m as its own benchmark. [Alpine Première launch dossier](https://www.renaultownersclub.com/wp-content/uploads/vehicles/Alpine%20A110%20-%202017%20Launch.pdf) · [Quattroruote test PDF](https://www.quattroruote.it/prove/2018/alpine_a110_la_prova_della_premiere_edition/_jcr_content/summaryParsys/article_attachment/file.res/Centro-Prove-Alpine-A110.pdf)
- Alpine Australia lists **8.61 / 13.14 / 18.38 / 24.09 / 30.05 / 36.89 / 44.01 km/h per 1,000 rpm** and **205/40 R18 front, 235/40 R18 rear**. This early 252 PS A110 technical table gives an effective-gearing reference. With an independently justified loaded rear-tire circumference `C` in metres, the total gear × final-drive reduction is `60 × C / (km/h per 1,000 rpm)`. If used for a provisional model, label these derived effective ratios and keep the individual gear and final-drive values unknown. The fixed loaded-circumference approximation should be documented. [Alpine Australia technical table](https://www.australiancar.reviews/_pdfs/Alpine_A110_XEF_TechnicalSpecifications_201806.pdf)

### 2021 Porsche Macan GTS — PDK gears verified, axle ratio unresolved

- Porsche's pre-facelift US table and period EU factory sheet agree on **3.69 / 2.15 / 1.41 / 1.03 / 0.79 / 0.63 / 0.52**. The US table lists **383 lb-ft @ 1,750–5,000 rpm** for the 375 hp **GTS**. **405 lb-ft** belongs to the **Turbo** in that sheet. MotorTrend tests the 375 hp GTS. Don't use the 440 PS facelift (whose sheet is dated 08/2021) to set engine or tire data for a US MY2021 GTS. [Porsche pre-facelift US technical sheet](https://download.newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr%3A345230e8-84c0-47c8-9c9f-864353db1751/2020_Macan_Technical_Data.pdf) · [Porsche pre-facelift EU GTS sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr%3A7c0395be-0ed6-4cbd-be56-d6f2c6abbbaf/PAG_MacanGTS_TD_EN.pdf)
- Porsche EU lists a **4.67 rear axle**. The US MY2021 C/D spec database reports **4.13**. Since the early US Porsche table leaves the axle out, keep the conflict visible until the US axle code or appropriate documentation resolves it. MotorTrend tested **4,479 lb (55/45)**, **3.9 s to 60 mph**, and **12.6 s at 107.8 mph**. [MY2021 US secondary spec listing](https://www.caranddriver.com/porsche/macan-gts/specs/2021/porsche_macan-gts_porsche-macan-gts_2021) · [Original MotorTrend test](https://www.motortrend.com/reviews/2021-porsche-macan-gts-first-test-review)

## Items that still need evidence

The sheets usually cover rated output, gearing, curb mass, tires and some aero. Exact model-year Cd for several cars, proprietary inverter/battery discharge maps, boost and torque curves, clutch behavior and time-varying torque splits remain open. Keep separate labels for catalog values, assumptions, factory claims and measurements. Where the current physics cannot reproduce a test or a powertrain, leave its result browsable and keep the simulation gated. Use the original test's stated rollout correction rather than assuming the same correction for each publisher or year.

PR #3 reports 48 of the 73 new cars playable. Exact factory gearing for the Integra Type R, GT500, 720S and 911 Turbo S gives four candidates for the existing manual or dual-clutch models, subject to implementation and calibration checks.
